# Acceptable Use

Raemote is for reaching applications **you own or are authorized to access**, on
machines **you control or are authorized to use**.

Please don't:

- use Raemote to gain unauthorized access to systems or data;
- use it to distribute malware, or to attack or scan third parties;
- abuse shared relay infrastructure (for example bulk transfer, denial of
  service, or circumventing its limits).

Notes:

- The default relay and discovery infrastructure is operated by
  [n0](https://www.iroh.computer/) under their terms; Raemote does not control it.
- If you run a server, you are responsible for the apps you expose and for
  revoking devices you no longer trust (`raemote devices revoke`).
- We may rate-limit or block abusive use of any infrastructure we operate.
